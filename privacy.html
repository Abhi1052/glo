Chamku privacy (test version)

Chamku is a bedtime-story app for young children. It is being tested with a small group of parents. This page says what we collect and why, in plain words.

What we collect

  • Your email address (and your name, if you sign in with Google). Signing in is optional. It lets you stay signed in on your phone.
  • Which stories are played and how far: for example "story X, stopped at 3 minutes" or "finished". This tells us which stories children enjoy and which ones we should replace. If you have not signed in, this is saved under a random number for your phone, with no name or email.
  • Sign-in steps: the type of phone and browser, and which sign-in step worked or failed. No name or email. This helps us fix sign-in problems.
  • Your 1–10 feedback, if you choose to send it.

What stays on your phone only

Your child's name and age, your favourite stories and your settings are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We never sell or share your email.
  • We don't collect anything about your child except which stories were played.

Where it is stored

Sign-in and play records are stored with Google Firebase (servers in India, Mumbai region). Feedback answers are stored in a Google Sheet that only the Chamku team can open.

Deleting your data

Email us and we will delete your account and play records: as29110@gmail.com
